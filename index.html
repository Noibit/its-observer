// What Observer watches

The signals an attacker would chase, watched on your behalf.

Observer is fully managed - we run it for you, watching continuously so your team can focus on the findings instead of the monitoring.

Credential exposure

Leaked passwords surfacing in breaches and paste sites, matched back to your domains before they're weaponized.

Typosquatting

Look-alike and typo variants of your domains, surfaced before a near-identical address can be used to fool your users.

Real-time impersonation

Freshly-issued certificates scored against your domains in real time, so a look-alike hostname spun up to impersonate you is flagged the moment it appears.
